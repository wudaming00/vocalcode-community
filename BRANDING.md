# VocalCode name and brand

The source licence and the identity of an official project are different
things. AGPL-3.0-only grants copyright permissions; it does not grant trademark
rights in the VocalCode name or logo. This document does not claim that any
mark is registered, add a noncommercial restriction, or reduce AGPL rights.

## Refer to the project accurately

You may truthfully describe compatibility, provenance, reviews, and changes,
for example: "An independent fork of VocalCode" or "Based on VocalCode".
Preserve the copyright and licence notices required by the applicable licences.
Commercial activity is not, by itself, misuse of the brand.

Do not imply that an independent build, service, support offer, or modified
version is maintained, endorsed, signed, or sold by the VocalCode maintainer
when it is not. Do not present a third-party payment/support channel as the
official project's channel.

For independent forks we recommend a distinct project name and icon, with
clear attribution to VocalCode. That recommendation is not a new copyright
licence condition, a ban on forks, or a requirement to obtain permission to
exercise rights granted by AGPL. This policy does not restrict lawful fair
or nominative use of the name or override applicable law.
