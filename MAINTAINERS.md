## Why work together here?

The useful shared resource is not just the code: it is a maintained set of
reproductions, multilingual examples, native audio/focus tests, and documented
decisions. Bringing fixes here lets future users benefit from the same tests
and reduces duplicated debugging. Forking remains a valid AGPL right; nobody
is required to submit their changes upstream.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for the next priorities. Small, reviewable work
with clear reproduction steps is more useful than a large untested rewrite.
Major architecture/model changes should be discussed before implementation.
The maintainer reviews merges; additional maintainers and decision procedures
can be documented if the project grows. No governance role is promised here.

## Support expectations

Support is asynchronous and best-effort. There is no guaranteed
response time, fix date, private onboarding, or service-level agreement.
Report with app version, OS, CPU, model, device, reproduction steps,
and synthetic data. Never include a real meeting or credential by default.

This does not announce a cancellation or change of previous purchase terms.
Existing commercial/customer obligations require a separate review and
communication; an open-source announcement is not a substitute for that.
