Bring your words with you

Local migration · preview first · keep existing entries

Import a file or paste a list copied from your other app’s dictionary screen. No account sign-in or upload. Wispr Flow has no built-in dictionary export, but a visible list may still be transferable. Direct screen/database adapters need version-specific verification and are not connected yet. This does not import subscriptions or private history.

Up to 3 MiB / 1,000 incoming entries; the dictionary currently holds at most 512 rules. A single-word entry normalizes spelling/case; it does not retrain the speech model. Exports are readable JSON—store them privately.

Voice snippets

With progressive insertion off, say the whole phrase “snippet name” or “插入词块名称”. Single-line snippets insert verbatim on release. Multiline snippets are copy-only; never automatically sent or executed. Snippets are stored locally, like your dictionary.

Upcoming calendar meetings · optional Beta

Google Calendar metadata goes directly between this device and Google. Recording audio and notes are not sent. Read-only, one account/calendar at a time, upcoming 7 days; all-day and declined events are omitted. A calendar event does not mean you have joined or consented to record.

Select an event to suggest a title; then review sources and press Start meeting yourself. After recording, select a saved meeting below and load its association before linking it. Snapshots keep their original title/time when Google events change.

Disconnect removes local authorization and upcoming-event cache; saved meeting associations remain. To revoke Google's grant too, remove VocalCode in your Google account permissions.
